What we offer

Custom-designed Solar Power Systems

We design a solar power system tailored to your building, its energy consumption and the available installation options.

Inverters

Every solar power system includes an appropriately selected inverter to ensure efficient use of the energy produced. We work with proven manufacturers SolaX and GoodWe.

Turnkey Solar Power Systems

Technologies we work with

SolaX

Smart use of the energy you produce

SolaX inverters and battery systems help homes and businesses get the most out of the energy they produce. With highly efficient inverters, the option to store surplus energy in batteries, and clear performance monitoring in an app, you have the whole system under control.

NORD

Durability and long service life

NORD solar power systems are built for durability and a long service life, and they run reliably even in demanding conditions. They’re a great choice if you’re looking for a stable, long-lasting solution with minimal maintenance needs.

GoodWe

Modern technology for smart control

GoodWe’s modern solutions can intelligently manage the flow of energy between solar panels, battery storage and the electrical grid. Hybrid inverters optimise the use of the energy produced, and selected systems can also be integrated with smart home control systems.

How We Work Together

  1. Consultation and Design

    We discuss your requirements and assess the technical possibilities of your property, then design a suitable solar power system and select the right technologies for the installation.

  2. Installation

    We install the solar panels, inverter and batteries and complete all electrical work required for reliable operation of the system.

  3. Commissioning and Ongoing Service

    Once installation is complete, we test the system, put it into operation, and on request also provide ongoing service and maintenance.

Questions & Answers

Who is solar power suitable for?

Solar power is suitable for both homes and businesses. We always design a specific solution based on your building, its energy consumption and the available installation options.

Can I monitor my solar power system’s output in an app?

Yes. Selected technologies let you monitor the output and performance of your solar power system through an app.

Can a solar power system be combined with a battery?

Yes. Battery storage lets you store surplus energy produced during the day and use it later, whenever you need it.

Do you also provide ongoing servicing?

Yes. Our services also include ongoing servicing of the solar power systems we install.
