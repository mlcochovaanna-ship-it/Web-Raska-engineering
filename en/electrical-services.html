Electrical Inspections

Electrical inspections help verify the safety and technical condition of an installation and catch faults that could lead to equipment failure, electric shock or fire. After the inspection you’ll receive a report assessing the condition of the equipment, with any recommended repairs.

Electrical inspections are also required by law as part of workplace and operational safety.

Questions & Answers

When is an electrical inspection needed, and when is it mandatory?

Inspections check the safety and technical condition of an electrical installation. In businesses and industrial facilities, regular inspections are required by law as part of workplace safety. For family houses and flats used solely for residential purposes, a regular inspection isn’t required by law, but it becomes mandatory after a new installation, a major renovation, or if your insurer requires it.

Do you also carry out inspections in businesses and industrial facilities?

Yes. Inspections also cover commercial and industrial buildings, cabling, machinery and production lines.

What does a lightning protection system do, and why is it important?

A lightning protection system protects a building and its occupants from the effects of a direct lightning strike. It safely conducts the lightning current into the ground and significantly reduces the risk of fire or damage to the building.

What do I get after an inspection?

After the inspection you’ll receive an inspection report describing the condition of the installation and any recommended repairs.

Do you also inspect lightning protection systems and electrical appliances?

Yes. We also inspect lightning protection systems, power tools, appliances and devices.

Can installing solar power affect an existing lightning protection system?

Yes. A solar power system can significantly affect a building’s existing lightning protection, so panels can’t simply be installed on the roof without assessing how they relate to the air-termination system and down conductors. We take a comprehensive approach to protecting the building, including suitable surge protection for the electrical installation and the solar power technology.
