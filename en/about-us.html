Our Story

Our roots lie mainly in industry. Years of working with production equipment, electrical installations and technical systems gave us a broad perspective, which we now put to use on projects for homes, businesses and manufacturing facilities.

Over time we expanded our services to include complete electrical installation work, inspections and solar power systems. Electrical services and solar power now make up the core of our work.

Our industrial experience remains an important part of the company. It includes servicing injection moulding and extrusion equipment, pneumatic and hydraulic systems, cleaning water and cooling circuits, and manufacturing and repairing heating elements.
